Mental Health Awareness

Understanding mental health is an important part of a healthy life.

What is Mental Health?

Mental health is an important part of our overall well-being. It affects how we think, feel, learn, communicate and deal with everyday challenges. Taking care of our mental health can help us manage stress, maintain relationships and handle difficult situations in a healthier way.

1. Mental Stress

Mental stress can occur when we feel that the demands placed on us are difficult to manage. Students may experience stress because of exams, assignments, academic pressure, deadlines or expectations. Recognizing stress early can help a person take healthy steps to manage it.

2. Emotional Well-being

Emotional well-being means being able to understand and express our feelings in healthy ways. Talking to someone we trust, spending time with supportive people and taking care of ourselves can help us manage difficult emotions.

3. Sleep & Rest

Sleep and rest are important for concentration, learning, mood and everyday functioning. Maintaining a regular sleep routine and giving yourself enough time to rest can support good mental well-being.

4. Social Support

Having supportive people around us can make challenges easier to handle. Friends, family members and trusted adults can listen, encourage us and provide support when we need it.
